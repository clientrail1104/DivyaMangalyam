Permanent Safety Notice

Never send money to an online match.

DivyaMangalyam will never ask you to send money to another member to continue a relationship or secure a marriage.

Never share OTPs, passwords, banking credentials, card information, investment information or financial account details.

Truthful Information

Honesty builds trust.

Members must confirm that their profile, photographs, qualifications, employment details, family information and other information are true, accurate, current and complete to the best of their knowledge.

False, misleading, fraudulent or intentionally inaccurate information may result in suspension, removal or termination.

Important

A platform, not a promise.

DivyaMangalyam is a matchmaking platform and does not guarantee that a member will find a partner, receive a response, establish a relationship, become engaged, or get married. Purchasing a subscription provides access to the features and contact allowance specified in the selected package. Contact information remains subject to member privacy preferences, consent requirements, platform policies and applicable law. Members should independently verify information and exercise appropriate caution before sharing personal information, meeting another member, entering a relationship, financial transaction or marriage.
